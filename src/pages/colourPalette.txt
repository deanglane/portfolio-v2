🎨 Day-Off Café Color Palette
Name	Hex	Mood	Usage
Oat Cream	#FFF6E5	Light, airy, café wall	Main background (default body color)
Espresso Brown	#2B1A1A	Deep, roasted base tone	Primary text / strong accents
Toasted Walnut	#8B5E34	Warm highlight	Headings, icons, buttons, borders
Carrot Orange	#F77F00	Zesty energy	Primary CTA button / hover states
Burnt Caramel	#E76F00	Depth variant	Button hover, active states
Sage Leaf	#A3B18A	Calming, organic	Subtle accent backgrounds / highlights
Café Foam	#FAEEDC	Soft surface contrast	Card backgrounds / alternate sections
Dark Roast	#5C4033	Cozy text shade	Secondary text / paragraphs
Cinnamon Dust	#E6D5C3	Gentle neutral	Borders, dividers, low-contrast outlines
☕ How to Use Each
1. Background layers

#FFF6E5 (Oat Cream) → main body background (warm light base).

#FAEEDC (Café Foam) → alternate section background for depth changes (like “Today’s Specials”).

You can use a subtle paper texture overlay for both for tactile warmth.

2. Typography

Headings: #8B5E34 (Toasted Walnut) – rich but not harsh.

Body text: #5C4033 (Dark Roast) – readable on cream backgrounds.

Links / Emphasis: #F77F00 (Carrot Orange) – for highlight phrases like “See the Menu.”

3. Buttons & Calls-to-Action

Primary button: #F77F00 background, white text.

Hover: #E76F00 (Burnt Caramel).

Outline button: white or transparent with border #8B5E34 and text same color.

4. Cards / Containers

Card background: white (#FFFFFF) or #FAEEDC.

Card border: #E6D5C3 (Cinnamon Dust).

Card text: #5C4033.

Card icon: #8B5E34.

Optional accent line or left edge strip in #A3B18A (Sage Leaf) to hint at the “recipe index” vibe.

5. Icons & Decorative Elements

Mix #8B5E34, #F4A261 (optional light orange variant), and #A3B18A.

Keep opacity ~40% for background floating icons so they feel airy.

6. Footer

Background: #FAEEDC

Text: #8B5E34 with ~80% opacity for a mellow finish.
